Why “Agentic” Now for TM&D?

TM&D already leads the industry in distribution reach and digital foundations. The next leap is closing the gap between seeing an issue on a dashboard and executing the fix across systems.

01 · Today's Strength

Unmatched Scale, Synergy & Digital Spine

  • ~7M Outlet Reach: Direct servicing backed by integrated CFA & stockist portals and live analytics.
  • Omnichannel Portfolio: Foods, Personal Care, Stationery & Tobacco moving across GT, MT & Q-Com on one backbone.
  • Deep Route-to-Market & Trade Trust: Multi-tier CFA, Wholesale Dealer & rural sub-stockist network delivering unmatched urban and rural assortment depth.
02 · The Execution Bottleneck

Insight Is Automated; Action Is Still Manual

  • Cross-System Stitching: Dashboards flag issues, but humans still bridge fixes across ERP, TMS/WMS, CFAs & WDs.
  • Silent OSA & SLA Erosion: Beat gaps, >75% MT shelf-life rules, dock penalties & manual claim audits silently hit sales and margin.
  • Frontline Admin Drag: Reps & ASMs lose prime market hours to cluttered SFA screens, 8:30 AM MIS prep & photo/POD uploads.
The Paradigm Shift in Operational AI
Era 1 · Past

Traditional Automation
