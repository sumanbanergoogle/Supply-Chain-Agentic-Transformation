How Supply Chain Agents Are Built

One spectrum on Gemini Enterprise: business teams build No-Code Gems in minutes, operations architects compose Low-Code workflows in Agent Studio, and engineers extend complex ERP/SCADA logic using ADK.

Tier 1 · No-Code

Gemini Gems

Plain-English instructions + Google Drive/Sheets grounding. Built by Planners, KAMs, and Category Managers in 15 minutes.

Examples: NPI Demand Profiler, JBP Negotiation, SKU Pruning
Tier 2 · Low-Code

Agent Studio

Visual canvas connecting reasoning loops, BigQuery data marts, and OpenAPI connectors to SAP, WMS, and TMS.

Examples: PO Expediting, Safety Stock, Dynamic Rerouter
Tier 3 · Multimodal

Vision & OCR

Built-in Gemini image/video understanding for shelf audits, line defects, BOLs, and damaged returns—zero custom ML training.

Examples: Visual Defect Triager, Return Fraud Sentinel
Tier 4 · Pro-Code

Agent Dev Kit (ADK)

Full Python/Go orchestration for multi-agent S&OP war-rooms, real-time SCADA loops, and deterministic optimization solvers.

Examples: Digital War-Room, Disruption Re-sequencer
