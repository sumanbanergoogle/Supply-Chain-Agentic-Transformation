From Personas to Production

Enterprise Governance, Policy & Human Approval

Supply chain agents never execute unapproved financial commitments or food-safety overrides. Every action is identity-bound, policy-checked, and logged.

1 · Identity & RBAC

Agents inherit the user's exact ERP/SAP permissions. A regional buyer only sees regional POs; margin tables remain restricted to Finance.

2 · Human-in-the-Loop Gates

Routine reconciliations run autonomously; high-value STOs, QA batch releases, and carrier claims pause for one-click human sign-off.

3 · Zero Data Leakage

Enterprise data never trains public models. VPC Service Controls and CMEK keep supplier contracts and pricing inside your perimeter.

4 · Full Audit Lineage

Every recommendation stores its cited documents, BigQuery snapshot timestamp, and approver ID for regulatory and ISO audits.
