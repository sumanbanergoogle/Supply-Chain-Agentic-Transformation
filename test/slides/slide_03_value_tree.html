The TM&D Value Tree

After India’s supply chain spine comes ITC Trade Marketing & Distribution (TM&D): we anchor on the 4 operational & P&L levers within TM&D’s execution span across General Trade, Rural, Modern Trade, and Quick Commerce—grounded in global On-Shelf Availability (OSA) benchmarks.

ITC TM&D North Star Outcome (Bounded to Execution & Availability)

Profitable Revenue Growth & Capital Efficiency across TM&D

Role Scope vs. Brand Managers: Brand teams own product mix & pricing; TM&D owns physical shelf availability, outlet execution, trade spend effectiveness, and cost-to-serve.

Lever 01 · Execution Assortment & Availability

On-Shelf Availability & Beat Assortment

Eliminating Kirana zero-billing, improving outlet line-count (LSPC), and speeding new-launch execution across GT, Rural, MT, and Q-Com.

↑ Secondary Sales & LSPC
Lever 02 · Gross Margin

Trade Spend & Margin Effectiveness

Moving from blanket schemes to hyper-targeted promotions—eliminating window-display leakage and boosting incremental ROI per rupee of trade spend.

↑ Promo ROI & Realization
Lever 03 · Cost-to-Serve

Distribution & Logistics Cost Efficiency

Higher truck cube/weight fill rates, lower freight % of sales, reduced MT/Q-Com fill-rate penalties, and lower expiry & write-offs.

↓ Freight % & OTIF Fines
Lever 04 · Cash & Productivity

Working Capital & Field/Back-Office Productivity

Faster distributor cash-to-cash, automated claim & freight settlement, and more selling time for field reps and managers.

↓ DSI & ↑ Selling Hours
