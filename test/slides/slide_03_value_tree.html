The ITC TM&D Value Tree

Before exploring workflows or technology, we anchor every Agentic AI initiative on the 4 commercial levers that define ROI in CPG Logistics & Distribution.

North Star Outcome

TM&D EBITDA, Cash Flow & Market Share

Measured across General Trade, Modern Trade, Quick Commerce & CFAs
Lever 01 · Growth

Revenue Protection & Channel Compliance

Higher On-Time In-Full (OTIF), fewer lost sales from kirana stock-outs, and lower Modern Trade / Quick Commerce fill-rate penalties.

KPI: OTIF % · OOS Reduction · MT/Q-Comm Fines
Lever 02 · Cost

Logistics Cost Optimization

Lower freight cost as a % of sales via higher truck fill rates, reduced spot-freight premiums, and lower detention & demurrage.

KPI: Freight % of Sales · FTL Fill % · Detention
Lever 03 · Cash & Quality

Working Capital & Freshness (Foods / FMCG)

Lower Days Inventory Outstanding (DIO) across CFAs and stockists, and reduced expiry write-offs via FEFO-aware rebalancing.

KPI: CFA/WD DIO · SLOB & Expiry Write-Offs
Lever 04 · Productivity

Commercial & Control Tower Productivity

Automated freight audit, POD verification, and trade claim resolution—freeing field and operations teams from manual firefighting.

KPI: Claim Cycle TAT · Freight Leakage · Hrs Saved
